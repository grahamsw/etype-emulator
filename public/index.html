About E-Type

There's a theory that there are two stages to writing: first drafts, where you get everything out without overthinking, and editing, where you can overthink as much as you want.

This app is for first drafts.

Log in with your Google ID and give it permission to save things to a folder in your Google Drive. (That's all it does. All the code for this is in your browser.)

You can't backspace, you can't delete, you can't move the cursor around. It's a typewriter. Write your draft, save it to Google Drive, and edit it there.

Enjoy.
